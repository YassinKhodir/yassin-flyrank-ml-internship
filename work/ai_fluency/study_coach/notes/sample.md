# Artificial practice notes
These examples are invented for testing. They are not a professor's notes.

## Data leakage
Data leakage means the model sees information that would not be available when making a real prediction. Example: using next week's clicks to predict this week's result. Separate past training data from future test data.

## CTR
CTR means click-through rate. Divide clicks by impressions, then multiply by 100 for a percentage. With 10 clicks and 100 impressions, CTR is 10%.
